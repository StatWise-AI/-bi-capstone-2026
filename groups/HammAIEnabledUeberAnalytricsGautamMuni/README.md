#AI-Enabled Uber India Operations Analytics Dashboard Using Power BI
Project Overview
The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that enables Uber India to monitor and analyze key operational metrics, including ride bookings, revenue, cancellations, vehicle performance, customer behavior, ratings, and location-based demand. The dashboard will support data-driven decision-making by presenting meaningful insights and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) through an interactive reporting platform.
Business Problem
Ride-hailing companies generate a large volume of operational data every day from bookings, customers, drivers, vehicles, payments, and ride locations. However, analyzing this data using traditional reports or spreadsheets makes it difficult to identify business trends, operational inefficiencies, and customer behavior. This project aims to consolidate operational data into a centralized Power BI dashboard that provides real-time business visibility and supports strategic decision-making.
Project Objectives
The dashboard will be designed to answer important business questions such as:
How many rides are successfully completed and how many are cancelled?
Which vehicle category generates the highest revenue?
What are the major reasons for ride cancellations?
Which pickup and drop locations have the highest booking demand?
What are the busiest operating hours?
How satisfied are riders and drivers based on ratings?
How can AI-generated insights support operational decision-making?
Technology Stack
The project will be developed using:
Microsoft Excel for the operational dataset.
Power Query for data cleaning and transformation.
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for KPI calculations.
Power BI for creating an interactive dashboard with dynamic visualizations and business insights.
AI Integration for generating business insights and recommendations.
Expected Deliverables
The final solution will include:
A cleaned and structured operational dataset.
An interactive Power BI dashboard featuring ride operations KPIs and performance metrics.
AI-generated business insights and recommendations.
A presentation summarizing the business problem, methodology, findings, and recommendations.
